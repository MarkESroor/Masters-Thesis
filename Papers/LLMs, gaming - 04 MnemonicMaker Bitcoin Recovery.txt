Title: MnemonicMaker: A Serious Game for Reconstructing Bitcoin Wallet Mnemonic Phrases
Citation: Vlahavas et al., 2025, International Journal of Serious Games.
Source: https://journal.seriousgamessociety.org/index.php/IJSG/article/view/911
PDF in Papers: Yes — MnemonicMaker Serious Game for Bitcoin Recovery Phrases.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
MnemonicMaker turns BIP-39 wallet recovery phrases into routes in a game-like environment. The study compares this spatial-mnemonic game with traditional memorization for remembering recovery phrases.

Most important findings:
- Thirty-eight participants took part in a two-month study. After 60 days, the game group had significantly better phrase-recovery success than the control group.
- The sample was small and relatively homogeneous, and no real funds were at stake, so the result should not be taken as a security guarantee.

Future work:
The authors suggest testing other mnemonic tools, larger and more varied groups, phrases longer than 12 words, and standardized usability measures.
