Title: Enhancing Recognition Memory in Virtual Memory Palaces Using Worlds-in-Miniature
Citation: Junhyeok Lee and Kang Hoon Lee, 2025, Applied Sciences, 15(5), 2304. DOI: 10.3390/app15052304
Source: https://doi.org/10.3390/app15052304
PDF in Papers: Yes — Enhancing Recognition Memory Using Worlds-in-Miniature.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The authors combine a procedurally generated VR memory palace with a Worlds-in-Miniature overview map. The map shows the whole palace and the learner’s position, with the goal of making unfamiliar virtual spaces easier to navigate while memorizing images.

Most important findings:
- Forty participants memorized 30 images and completed immediate, 7-day, and 14-day recognition tests.
- The WIM interface significantly helped immediate recognition, especially for people with lower initial performance. The difference between conditions narrowed over delayed tests, so the evidence for lasting benefit is weaker.

Future work:
The authors recommend larger samples, more realistic spaces and distinctive landmarks, multisensory cues, repeated learning, and tracking how teleportation affects spatial encoding.
