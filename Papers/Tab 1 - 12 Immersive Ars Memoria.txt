Title: Immersive Ars Memoria: Evaluating the Usefulness of a Virtual Memory Palace
Citation: Jan-Paul Huttner, Kathrin Robbert, and Susanne Robra-Bissantz, 2019, HICSS Proceedings.
Source: https://aisel.aisnet.org/hicss-52/cl/teaching_and_learning_technologies/10/
PDF in Papers: No — the official AIS page is accessible, but its PDF download endpoint returned 403 Forbidden.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The study tests both learning performance and users’ willingness to adopt a VR memory-palace tool. It uses an experiment plus correlation and regression analyses to examine whether learning success relates to perceived usefulness and intention to use.

Most important findings:
- The authors report significant correlations between learning success and factors related to users’ intention to use a virtual memory palace.
- The result concerns performance and adoption-related measures; correlation does not show that usefulness perceptions cause improved learning.

Future work:
The authors call for continued refinement of usability and accessibility to support practical, sustained use in learning contexts.
