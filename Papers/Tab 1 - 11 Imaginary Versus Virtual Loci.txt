Title: Imaginary Versus Virtual Loci: Evaluating the Memorization Accuracy in a Virtual Memory Palace
Citation: Jan-Paul Huttner, David Pfeiffer, and Susanne Robra-Bissantz, 2018, HICSS. DOI: 10.24251/HICSS.2018.037
Source: https://doi.org/10.24251/HICSS.2018.037
PDF in Papers: Yes — Imaginary Versus Virtual Loci.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
This study examines how the material to be learned should be represented in a virtual memory palace: shown directly in the environment or imagined by the learner, as in traditional Method-of-Loci use.

Most important findings:
- The authors’ stated conclusion favors embedding learning content in the environment when designing virtual versions of the technique.
- The abstract does not report an effect size; avoid turning this design recommendation into a broad claim that virtual loci always outperform imagined loci.

Future work:
The paper recommends further testing of how content placement and interaction design affect memorization accuracy.
