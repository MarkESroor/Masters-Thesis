Title: Simulating a Mixed Reality Memory Palace
Citation: Matthew Rossman, 2020, University of Massachusetts Amherst honors thesis.
Source: https://old.mattrossman.com/file/honors-thesis.pdf
PDF in Papers: No — the author-hosted PDF timed out during download.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The thesis describes a prototype that uses spatial-computing hardware to bring memory-palace cues into the user’s real environment. It also outlines a psychological experiment that could guide the design of immersive learning tools.

Most important findings:
- The prototype demonstrates a possible mixed-reality implementation, but the thesis does not report results from the planned controlled memory experiment.
- Treat it as a design and research-plan contribution, not evidence of improved recall.

Future work:
The proposed next step is to conduct the planned experiment and use its results to refine the mixed-reality experience.
