Title: The Influence of Item Properties on Association-Memory
Citation: Christopher R. Madan, Mackenzie G. Glaholt, and Jeremy B. Caplan, 2010, Journal of Memory and Language, 63, 46–63. DOI: 10.1016/j.jml.2010.03.004
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2010.03.004
PDF in Papers: Yes — imaginable words.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The study tests whether word imageability and frequency help paired-associate recall only by improving memory for individual words or also by strengthening the association between paired words.

Most important findings:
- Imageability primarily improved retrieval of the association between the paired words.
- Word frequency mainly improved retrieval of the target item itself. Forward and backward cue performance were strongly correlated, supporting holistic learning of pairs.

Future work:
The paper does not emphasize a single future-study agenda in its abstract. It is useful as background on how vivid visual representations can strengthen associations, but it is not a Method-of-Loci or AI study.
