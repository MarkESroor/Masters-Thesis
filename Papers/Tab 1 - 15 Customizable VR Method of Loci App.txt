Title: A Customizable Virtual Reality Application for Enhancement of Method of Loci
Citation: Randika Madhushan Perera, E. A. I. Priyanga, B. P. De Silva, K. R. R. Karunarathne, H. M. P. P. Jayarathna, and S. H. D. Senanayake, 2019.
Source: https://uvawellassa-uni.com/ircuwu2019/docs/Proceedings.pdf
PDF in Papers: A one-page conference poster abstract is saved as A Customizable Virtual Reality Application for Enhancement of Method of Loci.pdf. No full article-length PDF was available.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The authors describe a mobile VR memory-palace application built with Unity and Google Cardboard. It lets users store information along a palace path and is intended to make the technique more approachable through customization.

Most important findings:
- The available version is a poster abstract about a prototype; it does not report a controlled memory-performance evaluation.
- Claims about comfort or improved recall should therefore be treated as proposed benefits rather than measured findings.

Future work:
The authors planned to add user-selected palace templates and preferences, then evaluate the system in broader educational or therapeutic settings.
