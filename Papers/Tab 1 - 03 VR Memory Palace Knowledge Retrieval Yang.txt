Title: A Virtual Reality Memory Palace Variant Aids Knowledge Retrieval from Scholarly Articles
Citation: Yalong Yang et al., 2020, IEEE VIS / IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics.
Source: https://vis.cs.brown.edu/docs/pdf/Yang-2020-VRM.pdf
PDF in Papers: Yes — A Virtual Reality Memory Palace Variant Aids Knowledge Retrieval from Scholarly Articles.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The paper adapts the memory-palace idea to information from scholarly articles. It compares ordinary study, an image-based palace, and an immersive VR palace for learning facts from research abstracts.

Most important findings:
- The VR memory-palace condition improved later retrieval compared with the baseline study condition.
- It also showed a moderate advantage over the image-based palace. Participants wanted more control over how information and visual cues were arranged.

Future work:
The authors identify personalization as a key direction, including letting learners choose or adjust placements, organization, and palace features.
