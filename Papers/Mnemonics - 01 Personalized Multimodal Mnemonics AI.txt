Title: Designing Personalized Multimodal Mnemonics With AI: A Medical Student’s Implementation Tutorial
Citation: Noor Elabd et al., 2025, JMIR Medical Education, 11, e67926. DOI: 10.2196/67926
Source: https://doi.org/10.2196/67926
PDF in Papers: No — the publisher/PMC pages exposed the article text, but direct PDF downloads were blocked by the site's JavaScript/robot check.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The tutorial gives medical students a workflow for creating text mnemonics with ChatGPT and visual mnemonic images with DALL-E 3, including prompts, personalization, iteration, and quality checks.

Most important findings:
- Across six medical concepts, the authors report an initial acceptable-output rate of 85%, rising to 95% after refinement.
- This is a generation tutorial, not a human learning trial; it measures whether outputs met the authors’ criteria, not whether students remembered more.
- Human review is important because generated medical content may contain errors or unclear imagery.

Future work:
The authors call for work on ethical use, privacy, bias, and studies that measure whether these tools improve real learner outcomes.
