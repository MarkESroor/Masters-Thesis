Title: Fostering Vocabulary Memorization: Exploring the Impact of AI-Generated Mnemonic Keywords on Vocabulary Learning Through Anki Flashcards
Citation: Agnes and Srinivasan, 2024, World Journal of English Language.
Source: https://www.sciedupress.com/journal/index.php/wjel/article/view/25258
PDF in Papers: Yes — AI-Generated Mnemonic Keywords in Anki Flashcards.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
This study tests whether LLM-generated mnemonic keywords added to Anki flashcards help students learn English vocabulary compared with ordinary flashcards.

Most important findings:
- The study reports stronger vocabulary outcomes for the group receiving AI-generated mnemonic keywords after repeated practice.
- The evidence comes from a specific course context and short study period; it does not by itself establish durable gains across languages or learner groups.

Future work:
The authors point toward testing delayed retention and other learning contexts, and refining the quality and relevance of generated keyword associations.
