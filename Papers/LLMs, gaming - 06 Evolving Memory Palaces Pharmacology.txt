Title: Pharmacology Retention and Evolving Memory Palaces: How the Method of Loci Can Be Modified for Medical Students’ Pharmacology Learning
Citation: Zhu et al., 2024, Journal of Pharmacology and Experimental Therapeutics / ASPET meeting poster abstract.
Source: https://jpet.aspetjournals.org/article/S0022-3565%2824%2917619-7/fulltext
PDF in Papers: No full paper PDF found. The public item is a conference/poster abstract with limited methods and results.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The work describes adapting memory palaces to pharmacology so that drug classes, mechanisms, and adverse effects can be organized in an evolving spatial structure as learners add material.

Most important findings:
- The abstract reports encouraging pharmacology retention and recognition, but it does not provide enough public detail to verify the sample, effect size, or experimental design.
- Treat the result as preliminary poster evidence, not a fully documented efficacy trial.

Future work:
The abstract points toward fuller experiments with explicit pre/post and delayed-retention measures, comparison with standard study methods, and adaptation to other medical domains.
