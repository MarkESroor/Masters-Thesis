Title: VRCopilot: Authoring 3D Layouts with Generative AI Models in VR
Citation: Lei Zhang, Jin Pan, Jacob Gettig, Steve Oney, and Anhong Guo, 2024, ACM UIST. DOI: 10.1145/3654777.3676451
Source: https://doi.org/10.1145/3654777.3676451
PDF in Papers: Yes — VRCopilot_ Authoring 3D Layouts with Generative AI Models in.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
VRCopilot combines voice, pointing, and drawn wireframes with generative models so people can create and revise 3D room layouts while working inside VR.

Most important findings:
- Generative suggestions can help with inspiration, but automatic generation can reduce user agency and produce layouts with weaker circulation and daylighting.
- Scaffolded creation, where users sketch a structure and then refine it with AI, gave users more control than automatic generation. Manual co-creation produced the strongest agency and creativity ratings.
- This is relevant to generative interface design, not a memory or learning-outcomes study.

Future work:
The authors discuss applying the interaction ideas to other spatial authoring tasks and improving control and refinement of generated layouts.
