Title: Objects vs Images for Method of Loci in Mixed Reality: Comparing the Efficacy of Using 3D Objects Against 2D Images in the Method of Loci
Citation: Samuel Sandberg Bröms, 2024, KTH master's thesis.
Source: https://kth.diva-portal.org/
PDF in Papers: Yes — Objects vs Images for Method of Loci in Mixed Reality.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
This thesis compares memory-palace items represented as 3D models with items represented as 2D images in a mixed-reality task. It reports a between-subject study with 20 participants.

Most important findings:
- The study did not find a statistically significant memory difference between the 2D and 3D conditions.
- The small sample, restricted physical space, and limited set of objects mean that the null result does not establish that the formats are equivalent.

Future work:
The thesis recommends a larger study, testing more objects and space, adding interaction, animation or sound, and comparing mixed reality with virtual reality.
