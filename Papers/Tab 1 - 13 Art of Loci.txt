Title: The Art of Loci: Immerse to Memorize
Citation: Anne Vetter, Daniel Götz, and Sebastian von Mammen, 2020.
Source: https://downloads.hci.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/Vetter2020aa.pdf
PDF in Papers: Yes — The Art of Loci - Immerse to Memorize.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The paper presents a VR serious-game prototype in which learners can turn study material into visual mind maps and try mnemonic strategies, including memory palaces. It also discusses the learning principles behind the game’s design.

Most important findings:
- The main contribution is the prototype and its design rationale, not a controlled demonstration of better recall.
- It is useful as an example of how to lower the entry barrier to spatial mnemonics through guided interaction.

Future work:
The proposed next step is to evaluate whether the game helps novices learn and use the technique, and whether it improves delayed memory performance.
