Title: Walkable Graph: An Immersive Augmented Reality Interface for Performing the Memory Palace Method
Citation: Rocco Raso, Johannes Lahann, Peter Fettke, and Peter Loos, 2019, AMCIS Proceedings.
Source: https://aisel.aisnet.org/amcis2019/human_computer_interact/human_computer_interact/10/
PDF in Papers: No — the AIS page is accessible, but the PDF download endpoint returned 403 Forbidden.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The authors propose an AR interface that overlays graph paths on a real floor so learners can physically walk a route while associating information with locations. The paper outlines an experiment comparing this approach with a printed-medium control.

Most important findings:
- This is a proposal and interface description, not a completed experiment. It provides no measured evidence that walking the graph improves recall.
- Embodied movement is a rationale for the design, not a tested outcome in this paper.

Future work:
The proposed next step is the empirical comparison, including AR walking, VR navigation, and conventional study conditions.
