Title: A Large Language Model-Infused Platform for Blending Spaced Repetition and Immersion in Mandarin Vocabulary Acquisition
Citation: Hume, 2024.
Source: https://conference.pixel-online.net/ICT4LL/files/ict4ll/ed0017/FP/9480-ICT6929-FP-ICT4LL17.pdf
PDF in Papers: Yes — A Large Language Model-Infused Platform for Mandarin Vocabulary Acquisition.pdf.

Abstract (paraphrased):
The paper describes an LLM-assisted Mandarin learning platform that turns learner-provided text, audio, images, or spreadsheets into Anki flashcards. Cards can include examples, audio, etymology-based mnemonics, radicals, classifiers, and related words.

Most important findings:
- This is a system and design description; it does not report a controlled test showing that LLM-generated cards improve vocabulary retention.
- Its contribution is combining spaced-repetition scheduling with contextual and multimedia learning materials.

Future work:
The platform needs empirical evaluation against standard Anki or other learning approaches, including delayed retention, content accuracy, and learner experience.
